03 / HOW EARTH IS SCULPTED

Forces that shape terrain.

Explore the major processes behind Earth's landforms.

↗
PROCESS / 01

Tectonic uplift

Movement and collision of Earth's plates can fold, fracture and raise the crust, creating mountain belts, high plateaus and fault-block landscapes.

EXAMPLE Himalayas
⌁
PROCESS / 02

Weathering & erosion

Rock breaks down through physical and chemical weathering. Water, wind, ice and gravity then transport material and reshape slopes.

EXAMPLE Grand Canyon
▽
PROCESS / 03

Deposition

When flowing water, wind or ice loses energy, it can deposit sediment and build floodplains, deltas, dunes and alluvial fans.

EXAMPLE Ganges–Brahmaputra Delta
◉
PROCESS / 04

Volcanic activity

Lava, ash and other erupted material can build volcanic cones and broad plateaus. Magma also cools underground to form intrusive rock.

EXAMPLE Mount Fuji
